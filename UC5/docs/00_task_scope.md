# UC5 task scope and evidence boundary

UC5 models monitoring and safeguarding connected medical devices in a fully synthetic hospital network. The executable slice covers one patient monitor, network-flow collection, abstract device-health observations, explainable detection, continuity-constrained response, evidence handling, authorized redaction and sharing, and evidence-only reconstruction.

## Source discipline

- Proposal UC5 material supports the device-monitoring objective, participant roles, forensic readiness, secure sharing, and anonymized flow-data relevance.
- The roadmap and working template define the entity/process/mathematical-model deliverable pattern.
- Exact topology, traffic rates, thresholds, clinical criticality, backup status, response rights, retention, sharing authority, and dataset mappings are explicit assumptions pending expert confirmation.

## Prohibited scope

No live medical or network endpoint, EHR/PACS connection, real patient/device identifier, clinical measurement, diagnosis, treatment recommendation, therapy control, or operational isolation capability is implemented. Hidden simulation truth is restricted to evaluation and is never an investigator or sharing input.

## Completion evidence

Completion is demonstrated by validated schemas and relations, seven BPMN artefacts, deterministic baseline and compromised runs, negative experiment branches, a predeclared 100-sample uncertainty analysis, automated tests, versioned integration contracts, and a verified SHA-256 artifact manifest.
