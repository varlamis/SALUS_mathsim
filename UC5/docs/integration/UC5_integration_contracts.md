# UC5 integration contracts

The authoritative set is `contracts/integration/uc5_integration_contracts.yaml`. Eight versioned boundaries cover abstract flow and health inputs, registry context, detection/XAI, clinical-safety policy and T4.4 response, evidence/forensics, redacted sharing to T3.3/T3.4, and validation output to WP6 and UC stakeholders.

Stable concepts are UC5 identifiers, UTC timestamps, abstract units, pseudonymous device class, essentiality and backup flags, detector contributions, requested/applied response, safety rationale, evidence hashes and explicit missingness. Live endpoints, real clinical/device fields, authentication, transport, hospital deployment, legal authority and DS8 mapping remain TBD and cannot be inferred from this synthetic contract.
