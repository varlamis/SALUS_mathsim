# UC3 — Industrial cyberattacks and safety anomalies

## Scenario

A synthetic level-control vessel is observed by a sensor and controlled by a deterministic PLC through abstract pump/valve commands. HMI, engineering, historian, monitoring, SALUS detection/XAI, policy, twin, and forensic services are modelled as bounded abstractions. A synthetic adversary emits abstract discovery/fuzzing requests, unauthorized writes, and application load. No valid exploit packet is created.

## Objective and questions

Demonstrate whether the entity/process model can represent attack reach, distinguish cyber anomalies from faults, enforce safety-constrained mitigation, preserve evidence, and reconstruct qualified claims. Determine what was received, validated, alerted, requested/applied, capable of process effect, preserved, and left uncertain.

## Boundary

The process is a mathematical surrogate, not a reactor or partner plant. DS4/DS5 are optional future offline inputs and are not used in nominal results. No real endpoints, addresses, credentials, register maps, safety procedures, or operational topology are present.
