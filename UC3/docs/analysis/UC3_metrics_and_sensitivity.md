# UC3 metrics and sensitivity

Metrics cover attack generation/validation/detection, cyber-to-process reach, safety violations, process extrema, channel/queue/observation loss, integrity failures, and unauthorized disclosure. Undefined ratios remain null.

The 100-sample seeded design varies sensor noise/bias, channel loss, observation probability, detection threshold, and enforcement delay. Results are synthetic model evidence, not proposal KPI attainment. Proposal improvement KPIs require agreed baselines and WP6 measurement protocols.
