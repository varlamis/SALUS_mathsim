# UC2 -- passive detection and categorization of IoT devices

Three passive observer families cover a synthetic scene containing approved and
unapproved device classes. Observations can be lost or dropped by a finite
queue. Only observed metadata enters association and classification. An
unapproved, sufficiently confident hypothesis triggers an explainable alert and
a bounded non-RF response. Evidence is minimized and custody-linked; the
investigation never receives hidden device truth.

Acceptance requires zero active-RF actions, zero privacy disclosures, explicit
loss and ambiguity, deterministic equal-seed results, and fail-closed registry
and policy degradation.
